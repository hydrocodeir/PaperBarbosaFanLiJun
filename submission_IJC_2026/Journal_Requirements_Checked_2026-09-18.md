# International Journal of Climatology requirements checked on 2026-09-18

Official author guidelines: https://rmets.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/10970088/about/author-guidelines

- Article type: Research Article.
- Maximum length: 7,500 words, excluding figure/table captions and bibliography.
- Language: English.
- Main-file order: title page; abstract; keywords; main text; acknowledgements; conflict of interest; funding; permissions; author contributions; data availability; references.
- Title page: title without abbreviations, running title below 70 characters, full author names, affiliations, and email addresses.
- Abstract: no more than 300 words and no citations.
- Keywords: 3–8, with at least two preferably represented in the title.
- Main body: Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion and/or Conclusion.
- References: Harvard author–date style; free-format submission is accepted.
- Main document: MS Word or RTF is requested for review.
- Tables: editable, self-contained, with titles and footnotes.
- Figures: legends must define symbols, abbreviations, and units; colour-blind-accessible design is recommended.
- Graphical abstract: mandatory for Research Articles; article title, author names, corresponding-author asterisk, no more than 80 words or three sentences, and an image legible at 50 × 60 mm.
- Supporting Information: submit with the manuscript, preferably as one file; number items Figure S1, Table S1, and so forth, and cite them in the main text.
- Submission portal: https://authors.wiley.com/journal/JOC
- ORCID: required for the submitting author.
- Data sharing: a Data Availability Statement is required; repository links and persistent identifiers are expected when data are shared, and scripts should be archived whenever possible.
- Peer review: single blind.

This file summarizes the requirements used to assemble the package; the official webpage controls if requirements change.
